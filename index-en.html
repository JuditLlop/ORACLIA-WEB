What this space is

Oraclia is a shared space for looking, thinking, and creating meaning through an alphabet of 21 symbols. Their meaning comes not only from each symbol's definition, but also from how the symbols relate to one another and what emerges in conversation.

The symbols come into play when they help make a relationship visible or open up a possibility. They do not need to appear every time, nor do they make fixed claims about you. What emerges is an interpretation you can question, change, or explore from another angle.

We can think together, explore a question, write, seek another perspective, or test an intuition. You can ask for a particular form: a debate, a scene, a dialogue, a question, an aphorism, a scientific perspective, a surprise, silence… If you do not choose one, Oraclia will select the form that best expresses what you are working on.

The project

A place to bring questions, ideas, and possibilities into focus.

Some decisions do not need a quick answer. They need conversation, different perspectives, and enough distance to see what is happening.

Oraclia offers a framework for exploring a situation, revisiting an idea, and creating without closing down its meaning too soon.
